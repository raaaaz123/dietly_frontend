## 2. Embeddable calculators (built, ships with the next deploy)

Every calculator page now has "Add this calculator to your site" with a copy-paste snippet. The iframe loads `/embed/<tool>`, and the snippet carries a plain credit link to the tool page on the host site. That is a real, editorially placed backlink every time someone uses it.

How to seed it:
- Email 20 small fitness coaches or gyms whose sites have a "resources" or "tools" page. Pitch: "free macro calculator for your clients, no signup, no ads, one line of code."
- Post the macro and 1RM calculators in r/fitness's weekly self-promo thread and in coaching Facebook groups as free tools for coaches.
